# NBA luxury tax

Basketball soft-cap leagues use the progressive NBA tax rates from 2025-26 onward. Brackets scale from the 2023-24 cap; three tax-paying seasons in the preceding four trigger repeater rates. Non-taxpaying teams receive tax distributions. Finances and roster views use the same projected tax as the assessment. Other sports and cap systems retain their rules.

Validated against the CBA marginal-tax and repeater examples. Apron restrictions and owner evaluation are separate dependent PRs.

Source: https://nbpa.com/cba
