# Basketball player dossier

The player overview is composed around identity, cost and on-court production rather than two generic bio/rating panels.

- The identity stage uses the actual player image or generated face, the player's club crest, jersey number, availability and selected-season production. Career production is labeled separately for retired players. Current contract amount, expiration, remaining years and annual salary schedule stay visible. Free agents, prospects and retired players get appropriate status labels rather than an active-contract claim.
- The scouting report shows all fifteen existing basketball attributes in three columns, current overall/potential with changes, an attribute radar and five-position suitability from the same position model as Court & Depth. The radar is a labeled composite of displayed scouting attributes, not an estimate of real production. Hidden-rating challenges suppress the scouting report under the existing player-page rules.
- Personal information, original management/negotiation/compare actions, jersey history, notes, every statistics table, ratings history, salaries, awards, injuries and transactions remain available. Career anchors lead to the full record below the overview.

The page uses the shared arena artwork, club identity, gold numeric values and Inter/local GT America typography from the Arena presentation. Player portraits are never replaced by stock or unrelated generated photos.

Validation includes long names, real simulated production, contract schedules and durations, injuries, free-agent/retired/prospect states, hidden ratings, original edit/compare controls, and both appearances at 1536/1440/1280/1024/736/390/320px without clipped attribute or production groups, page overflow or JavaScript errors.
