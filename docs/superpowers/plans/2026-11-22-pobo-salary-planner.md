# POBO three-year salary planner

1. Project three salary years from the league's effective salary year using scheduled salaries, roster contracts and released salary. Report expiring agreements and the signed distance from the configured cap and aprons.
2. Add the projection to Team Finances. In Trade, compare the user's commitments before and after the players in the validated offer. Keep the existing trade restrictions and proposal flow in charge of legality.
3. Cover expiration, scheduled and legacy contracts, released salary, picks-only proposals and cap thresholds. Verify the production views on desktop and at a narrow viewport.

Future thresholds stay at their current league values. The planner assumes the signed commitments run through expiration and excludes future moves, cap holds, roster charges and tax projections.

This branch builds on the effective salary and trade matching logic in PR #25.
