# Contextual finances

The everyday roster and front office no longer repeat the full apron report and
ownership objectives. A neutral, single-line payroll/apron context links to the
team's Finances screen. The ownership brief, targets, projected tax, total cost,
apron margins and complete restrictions remain in that dedicated screen.

Salary-floor, apron, hard-cap, over-budget and restricted-pick warnings remain
visible when relevant. Guaranteed dismissal is always a visible alert. This is
a presentation change; calculations, difficulty and owner evaluation are intact.
The team-specific link on another club's roster opens that club's finances.

This supersedes UI_DESIGN_PLAN's initial decision to repeat an ownership brief
on the roster. The following separate delivery replaces the large club banner
with a compact masthead and basketball court/depth view.
